{{#if role:swe}}
### Software engineering
- Titles: Software Engineer Intern, SWE Intern, Software Development Engineer Intern, Backend / Frontend / Full-stack Intern
- Format: online assessment (data structures and algorithms), then one or two technical interviews, sometimes a behavioral round
- Prepare: a steady practice-problem habit, one project you can whiteboard end to end, system basics (APIs, databases, caching) for later rounds
- Portfolio: public repos with READMEs; a deployed project beats a slide
{{/if}}
{{#if role:pm}}
### Product
- Titles: Product Manager Intern, Associate PM Intern, Product Intern, Rotational Product Intern
- Format: behavioral and product-sense interviews (design a feature, improve a product, prioritize a roadmap), estimation, sometimes a light technical screen
- Prepare: a product-critique habit (one product a week), metrics vocabulary, three stories about shipping with engineers, one about saying no
- Postings cluster at large tech companies and are few; referral-first applies doubly here
{{/if}}
{{#if role:tpm}}
### Technical program
- Titles: Technical Program Manager Intern, Program Manager Intern, Engineering Program Intern
- Format: behavioral (ownership, ambiguity, conflict), program-sense (plan a launch, handle a slipping date), a technical conversation about something you built
- Prepare: stories with schedules, risks, and stakeholders in them; one artifact (a plan, a tracker, a status format) you can describe
{{/if}}
{{#if role:data}}
### Data / ML
- Titles: Data Science Intern, Machine Learning Intern, Applied Scientist Intern, Analytics Intern, Data Engineering Intern
- Format: SQL and statistics screens, a take-home or case, ML fundamentals for ML roles, coding for data engineering
- Prepare: SQL fluency, A/B test reasoning, one analysis you can explain from question to decision, the math behind the models you list
{{/if}}
{{#if role:design}}
### Design
- Titles: Product Design Intern, UX Design Intern, UX Research Intern, Interaction Design Intern
- Format: portfolio review, a design exercise (take-home or live), behavioral
- Prepare: a portfolio with two or three case studies that show process and outcomes, not only screens; a story about a design that changed after research
{{/if}}
{{#if role:solutions}}
### Solutions / SA
- Titles: Solutions Architect Intern, Solutions Engineer Intern, Customer Solutions Intern, Sales Engineer Intern, Forward-deployed Engineer Intern
- Format: behavioral, a customer-facing scenario (explain a technical choice to a non-technical stakeholder), a light technical screen, sometimes a presentation
- Prepare: one story about translating a requirement into something shipped, one about a stakeholder who changed their mind, a short demo you can give in five minutes
- Few coding assessments; a strong path for builders who interview better in conversation than on a whiteboard
{{/if}}
{{#if role:consulting}}
### Consulting
- Titles: Consulting Intern, Technology Consulting Intern, Business Analyst Intern, Summer Associate
- Format: case interviews (market sizing, profitability, structuring), behavioral fit, sometimes a group exercise
- Prepare: case frameworks practiced out loud with a partner, mental math, three fit stories (leadership, teamwork, impact)
- Watch application caps: some firms limit applications per rolling window
{{/if}}
{{#if role:research}}
### Research
- Titles: Research Intern, Research Engineer Intern, Research Assistant (summer), Applied Research Intern
- Format: a research talk or deep dive on prior work, technical interviews on fundamentals, a conversation with the hosting researcher
- Prepare: one piece of work you can present in ten minutes with a question you still have, reading list for the lab's recent papers, code that reproduces one result
- Postings are few and often informal; email the researcher with a specific question before applying
{{/if}}
