## How the calendar works

Large companies post 8 to 11 months before the start date and many close by the end of that posting window. Mid-size companies post 4 to 7 months before. Startups hire just in time, 1 to 4 months before. Online assessments start within weeks of a posting, and interview loops run continuously from the first posting wave to about two months before the start.

| Season starts in | Big-company posting peak | OA and interview wave | Mid-size wave | Startups, just in time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Summer (May / June) | August to November of the prior year | September to February | January to March | February to May |
| Spring (January) | May to September of the prior year | July to November | September to November | October to January |
| Fall (September) | December to April | February to June | April to June | June to August |

## Weekly rhythm

- **Mon**: pipeline review (`/pipeline`): deadlines in the next 14 days, stale applications, cold contacts
- **Tue / Thu**: new applications, 2 to 3 each day
- **Wed**: outreach, 3 referral or alumni messages (see [[strategy/outreach-playbook]])
- **Fri**: interview prep, 2 practice problems or one case, plus one story-bank rehearsal out loud
- **Sun**: plan the week around classes and deadlines
