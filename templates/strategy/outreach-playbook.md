# Outreach playbook

> [!tip] How to fill this
> Keep the rules, write your own templates. Every message sent gets a dated line in the person's note under `## Timeline`, and the person's `last_contact` moves, so `/pipeline` can flag the follow-up.

## The rules

1. **Specific beats everything.** Reference the person's actual work: a post, a talk, a product decision.
2. **Give before asking.** Lead with something useful from your own experience.
3. **Short.** 50 to 120 words. Three sentences is fine. No resume attached on first contact.
4. **One small, concrete ask.** A question answerable in two lines, or a 15-minute call with a proposed time.
5. **Never ask for a job or referral in the first conversation.** Ask about the work. Referrals come from people who have talked to you.
6. **Connection request with a note beats a cold long message.** Cheaper for them to accept, better signal.
7. **Cadence**: a few touches a week, sustained, beats a burst of thirty.
8. **Follow-up discipline**: one bump after 5 to 7 days. Then log the silence and move on.
9. **After any call**: same-day thank-you naming one specific thing from the conversation.
10. **Public engagement counts.** A substantive comment on someone's post is outreach.

## Anti-patterns

- Asking for a referral in the first message
- Attaching a resume unasked
- Messages that could have been sent to anyone
- Following up more than twice
