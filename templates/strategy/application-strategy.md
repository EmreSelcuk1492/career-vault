# Application strategy

> [!tip] How to fill this
> Set the volume goals once and let `/pipeline` hold you to them. The tier definitions decide how much effort each application gets; the funnel table tells you what to fix when the numbers stall.

## Principles

1. **Referral-first.** A warm introduction beats a stack of cold applications. For reach and target companies, look for a path in `people/` before applying. Never fabricate a connection.
2. **Volume floor, quality ceiling.** A fixed weekly minimum regardless of mood. Extra effort (tailored resume, cover note, direct outreach) goes to higher tiers.
3. **Every application is a note.** No application exists outside `applications/`. Status lives in frontmatter, so the funnel is computed, not maintained by hand.
4. **Track the funnel, fix the leak.** `/pipeline` shows conversion by stage. Low applied-to-OA means the resume. Low OA-to-interview means practice. Low interview-to-offer means the story bank.

## Tiers

| Tier | Definition | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **reach** | dream fit, low odds | referral before applying; everything tailored |
| **target** | strong mutual fit | tailored resume plus a referral attempt |
| **safety** | high odds, still a good term | base resume, fast apply |

## Funnel diagnostics

| Weak stage | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| applied to oa | resume, eligibility, or timing | rebuild the resume against [[profile/skills]]; apply earlier in the posting window |
| oa to interviewing | practice | daily practice problems or cases for two weeks |
| interviewing to offer | stories or technical depth | rehearse [[interviews/story-bank]] out loud; debrief every round the same day |
